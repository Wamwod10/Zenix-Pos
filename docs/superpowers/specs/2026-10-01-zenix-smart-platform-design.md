# Zenix Smart Platform Design

Preserve the existing Zenix visual language and working business flows while completing Customers/CRM and credit sales, fixing critical mobile responsiveness, and surfacing useful intelligence without adding sidebar complexity.

Customers owns customer financial profiles, receivables aging, credit limits, purchase history, loyalty foundation, and payments. POS/Sales creates credit sales and mixed initial payment; refunds reduce open receivables before cash is returned. Customer receivables use an auditable ledger plus FIFO payment allocations.

Smart functionality stays inside existing surfaces: global command search and quick actions in the layout, notifications for actionable events, Zenix Pulse and stock-runout forecasts on Dashboard, supplier price intelligence in Suppliers, saved table views in existing data modules, shift reconciliation/day-close intelligence in Shifts, and Activity Log for the universal audit timeline.

Responsive behavior must remain usable at 320–430px phones, tablet, and desktop. No horizontal page overflow, clipped product grids, unusable selects, or modal content escaping the viewport.
